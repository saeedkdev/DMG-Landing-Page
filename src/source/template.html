Positioning

One company. Two connected engines of growth.

DMG combines physical infrastructure with proprietary software and regulated digital-asset capabilities. This vertical integration allows the company to participate across the full digital infrastructure value chain.

Core — Data Center Infrastructure

Infrastructure engineered for high-density compute.

DMG designs, builds and operates its own sites. Transmission-grade power, a cool northern climate and liquid-cooling capability let the same campus serve AI training, high-performance computing, colocation tenants and Bitcoin mining.

Why now

The next era of infrastructure will be defined by access to power.

AI, high-performance computing and Bitcoin increasingly compete for secure, efficient and scalable energy infrastructure. DMG's combination of data-center assets, operational experience and proprietary software places the company inside that competition rather than alongside it.

Energy

Secured interconnection and cold-climate siting decide how quickly capacity can be delivered at all.

Compute

Density, cooling and uptime determine which workloads a site can actually host — from mining to AI training.

Digital assets

Software and settlement infrastructure turn operated compute into services institutions can use under audit.
